Other facts used in the story sample:
- Born 9 Sep 1985 in Zadar.
- Refugee during the Croatian War of Independence. His grandfather was killed in December 1991, and the family lived for seven years in the Hotel Kolovare in Zadar.
- 2018 Ballon d'Or, the first since 2007 not won by Messi or Ronaldo.
- 2018 World Cup: runner-up, Golden Ball. 2022 World Cup: third place.
